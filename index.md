**What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic this quarter was love–hate steering in LLMs. I find it striking that shifting a model's internal activations along a single direction, from hate toward love, can noticeably change what it generates without any retraining.

**Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would like to investigate whether steering an LLM's emotions can improve its accuracy. I would apply emotion-based steering directions to a model and compare its performance on benchmark question-answering tasks against the unsteered model, to see which emotions, if any, help.

I would use LLMs to cut out a lot of the busy work, such as writing boilerplate code and repetitive experiment scripts. That would free up time to investigate more approaches instead of spending most of the quarter on implementation.
